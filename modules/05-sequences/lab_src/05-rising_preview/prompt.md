A display previews the first k rising adjacent pairs from an integer reading feed. Implement `rising_preview(readings, k)` returning tuples `(previous, current)` wherever current is greater than its immediate predecessor in the original source. Keep occurrence order; equal values and falls are not results. k is between zero and 20. A finite source may contain fewer than k rises; an unbounded source is promised to contain enough when k is positive. Stop immediately on the kth rise, without requesting another reading. For k equal to zero, consume nothing. Call `show_preview(result)` once to draw the last returned pair and the result count. The starter is correct on finite inputs but consumes their entire source and cannot meet a live feed's contract.
