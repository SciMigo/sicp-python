A display previews a feed of integer readings by showing the first `k` rises: places where a reading is greater than the one immediately before it. Implement `rising_preview(readings, k)` returning those rises as `(previous, current)` pairs, in the order they occur. Equal readings and falls are not rises.

The feed may be endless. Stop as soon as you have the `k`-th rise, without asking for another reading; with `k` equal to 0, ask for none. A finite feed may run out first, and then you return the rises it had. `k` is between 0 and 20.

Each time you have a new reading and the one before it, call `show_step(previous, current, result)` after deciding whether it is a rise. Call `show_preview(result)` once at the end. The starter gives the right answer on a finite list, but it reads the whole feed first.
