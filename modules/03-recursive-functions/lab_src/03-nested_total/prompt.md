Write `nested_total(node, path=())`. A node is an integer or a list whose items are nodes, nested at most 10 deep. An integer's total is itself, an empty list's total is 0, and any other list's total is the sum of its children's totals. Visit children left to right with a recursive call each, passing `path + (i,)` for the child at index `i`. The starter calls `show_node(path, node, answer)` once per call, after the answer is known; keep that, so every integer, every list and every empty list gets one frame, children before their parent. Do not change the input or keep a running total outside the call.
