A shipping manifest can arrive in several record formats. Implement `manifest_report(records, weight_of, label_of)` returning total weight and the label of the first heaviest record. Empty input returns `(0, None)`; weights are nonnegative integers and labels are strings. The supplied operations are the only way to read a record. **Each record permits at most one call to weight_of.** Handle up to 500 records, preserve their order and contents, and keep invocations independent. Call `show_report(total, heaviest)` once after computing the answer. The starter produces a correct report, but exceeds the read allowance for nonempty input.
